Dear Aleena

Sab se pehle... Happy Birthday, Aleena. ❤️

Aaj tumhara birthday hai aur honestly, mujhe samajh nahi aa raha ke main tumhare liye exactly kya likhun. Kuch logon ke liye ek simple si birthday wish kaafi hoti hai, lekin kuch log aise hote hain jin ke liye jitna bhi likho, phir bhi lagta hai ke kuch kehna baqi reh gaya hai.

Aur tum unhi logon mein se ho.

Tum meri life ka sirf ek naam nahi ho. Tum un logon mein se ho jin ki presence ka ehsaas tab aur zyada hota hai jab life thori difficult ho jaye. Kabhi ek simple si conversation, kabhi ek choti si hansi, kabhi ek random si baat... aur kabhi sirf ye feeling ke "someone is there."

Maybe main har dafa express nahi karta, lekin I genuinely appreciate your presence in my life.

Tumhari friendship un cheezon mein se hai jise explain karne se zyada feel kiya jata hai. Zindagi mein bohat se log milte hain. Kuch sirf waqt guzarte hain, kuch memories ban jate hain, aur kuch log dil mein ek aisi jagah bana lete hain jahan waqt bhi asani se nahi pohanchta.

I hope tum hamesha meri life ke unhi beautiful chapters mein se ek raho.

Aleena, meri sab se sincere wish tumhare liye ye hai ke tumhein life mein sirf success na mile, balkay peace bhi mile. Sirf people na milein, balkay genuine people milein. Sirf love na mile, balkay woh love mile jo tumhein secure aur respected feel karaye.

May Allah tumhare har us dream ko poora kare jo tumne kabhi kisi ko bataya bhi nahi. Tumhari mehnat ko rang mile, tumhare parents tum par proud hon, aur tumhari zindagi mein woh khushiyan aayein jo tumne kabhi imagine bhi na ki hon.

Kabhi apni worth ko kisi aur ke behaviour se measure mat karna. Kisi ke stay karne ya leave karne se tumhari value kam nahi hoti. Tumhari worth tumhare heart mein hai, tumhari kindness mein hai, tumhari personality mein hai, aur un choti choti cheezon mein hai jo tum shayad khud notice bhi nahi karti.

Aur please... apni smile ko kabhi lose mat karna. Kuch smiles sirf face ko beautiful nahi banatin, balkay doosron ke difficult days ko bhi thora easy bana deti hain.

I hope tum hamesha woh Aleena raho jo apne dreams ke liye fight karti hai, jo apne people ka khayal rakhti hai, jo difficult days ke baad bhi smile kar leti hai aur jo apni life ko apne tareeqe se beautiful banane ki koshish karti hai.

I don't know future humein kis direction mein le jayega. Life change hogi, priorities change hongi, circumstances change honge. Shayad hum busy ho jayenge, shayad conversations kam ho jayengi, shayad distance aa jayega.

But I sincerely hope ke chahe waqt kitna bhi guzar jaye, tum kabhi ye na bhoolo ke tumhari life mein ek aisa dost tha jo tumhare liye genuinely good things wish karta tha.

Someone who wanted to see you happy.
Someone who respected you.
Someone who was proud of you.
And someone who was genuinely thankful that life introduced you to him.

Aaj ke din apni past mistakes ke bare mein mat sochna. Un logon ke bare mein mat sochna jo chale gaye. Un cheezon ke bare mein mat sochna jo abhi tak nahi mili.

Bas ek dafa peeche dekho aur dekho ke tum kitna aage aa chuki ho. You survived days you once thought would break you.

Aur ye khud mein ek achievement hai.

So here's my wish for you:

May you laugh more.
May you overthink less.
May you find peace.
May you become everything you dream about.
May you never settle for less than you deserve.
May your heart remain soft.
May your soul remain beautiful.
And may life always give you reasons to say: "Yes, it was all worth it."

Happy Birthday once again, Aleena. 🌸

I hope this new chapter of your life is softer, brighter, more peaceful and full of beautiful surprises.

Aur agar kabhi life bohat heavy lagne lage, to bas itna yaad rakhna: somewhere, someone is silently wishing the best for you. Always.

With respect, care & lots of duas. ❤️
